ESC/POS Command Examples

Initialize Printer (Base64)

G0A=
ESC @

Reset & Print "Hello" (Base64)

G0BGZWxsbyEK
ESC @ followed by "Hello!\n"

Feed Paper (Hex)

1b 64 05
ESC d (feed 5 lines)

Cut Paper (Hex)

1d 56 00
GS V (partial cut)
